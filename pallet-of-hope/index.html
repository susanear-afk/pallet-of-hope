Our Story

Born from Experience,
Driven by Heart

The Giving Pallet was founded by two clothing liquidators who know the industry inside and out — Lucy Li and Susan Kattamuri. Both women entered reverse logistics in 2023 and within three years built thriving businesses of their own. Along the way, they saw an opportunity to turn that success into something bigger: a way to hand other families the same tools that changed their lives.

Their two paths led to the same idea: donate clothing inventory directly to families and teach them how to resell it — creating flexible, work-from-home income that bends to their life, not the other way around.

Lucy Li
Co-Founder · Chicago, Illinois
Lucy is the owner of Lucy's Liquidation, based in Chicago, Illinois. Lucy's core operation is built on direct third-party logistics and retailer relationships. As a primary contract holder to several fashion brands, Lucy has deep ties across the supply chain — bringing the infrastructure and sourcing power that makes The Giving Pallet possible. Lucy built her business on relationships, and The Giving Pallet is her way of extending that same spirit of generosity to families who are ready to build something of their own.
"Success means more when you bring someone else with you."
Susan Kattamuri
Co-Founder · Los Angeles, California
Susan is the owner of SGV Deals, based in Los Angeles, California. She is raising three children with autism, each with different challenges — one of whom will require one-on-one support for life. She saw firsthand how impossible it is to hold a traditional 9-to-5 when autism means constantly juggling medical appointments, school needs, sensory emergencies, and therapy. Susan started reselling on Facebook Marketplace in 2019 on her own schedule, and in 2023 entered the liquidation industry with her first truckload purchase. What started as a few listings grew into pallets, then full truckloads. Having built something from nothing while raising three children with autism, Susan knows exactly what's possible when a family gets the right opportunity at the right time — and she's determined to be that opportunity for someone else.
"Living is giving."

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower low-income, single parent households or families raising children with special needs — especially those who face barriers to traditional employment due to the daily demands of caregiving — by donating clothing inventory and providing the business education needed to launch and grow reselling businesses. Because every family deserves a pathway to financial independence and a community that has their back, one pallet at a time.

Eligibility

Who Can Apply?

✅ You may qualify if you are:

  • A single parentHead of household raising children independently
  • Low income as determined by federal guidelinesBased on current HHS poverty guidelines
  • A parent of a child with autism or special needs
  • Unable to maintain traditional employment due to caregiving
  • Motivated to start or grow a reselling business
  • Willing to share your story and goals

📄 Accepted verification documents:

  • IEP (Individualized Education Program)
  • Letter from pediatrician, neurologist, or therapist
  • SSI/SSDI award letter for your child
  • Regional Center enrollment letter
  • EBT/CalFresh card or approval letter
  • Medi-Cal/Medicaid card
  • Most recent tax return (page 1 only)
  • Signed self-declaration letter

There are no wrong answers. We just want to know your heart and your story.
